**Everything in the tables below was recorded in the original audit on October 21, 2026. It is a historical note, not a current price list.** Nobody has re-checked it in this audit. Do not quote these prices, ratings or features on the site or in a pitch until each one has been looked up again.

Ratings were taken from Baselane's own blog and are directional only. G2 pages were not opened. Latchel's real pricing was not checked. Stessa, Innago and Rentec Direct were not researched in depth.

## The weak spot

Competitors have established vendor networks and human backup. GoHome's vendors are fictional. The question the product has to answer is: **who actually shows up to do the repair?** Until at least two real vendors agree to pilot it, any claim about repairs getting done is a claim about the demo.

## Claims that need fresh research before anyone repeats them

2. Every rating, and the sources (Capterra, Trustpilot, Baselane's blog).

## Recommended next step

Contact Hemlane and Latchel as a prospective customer. Ask for real pricing, how their sales process works, and how they handle vendors and emergencies. Write down what they say with the date. Do not record anything as verified until it was seen or heard directly.

Keep this research apart from code fixes. None of it changes the prototype.
